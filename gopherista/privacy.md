# Gopherista Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Gopherista doesn't collect personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and it doesn't send anything about you to me or to anyone else.

## What stays on your device

Your scripts, settings and saved documentation pages are stored only on your device. Your scripts also appear in the Files app, and your device backups include them. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

## Purchases

The free trial and the Full Version are bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment and doesn't share your name, Apple Account or payment details with me.

## Network

Gopherista itself connects only to Apple, for purchases. A script you write can connect to the internet, for example with Go's net/http package, but only while you run it and only to the addresses in your code. I don't see those connections or their data.

## Photos

When you save a canvas image, Gopherista asks for permission to add it to your photo library. It can't see your photos.

## Crash reports

If you share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send me crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you.

## Email

If you write to me, I use your email address and message only to reply to you, and I don't share them. Ask and I'll delete them.

## Children

Gopherista collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I'll post the new version on this page with a new date.
